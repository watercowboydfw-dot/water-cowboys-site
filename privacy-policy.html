Water Cowboys ("we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect when you use our website, why we collect it, and how we handle it.

Information we collect

When you use our water hardness lookup tool, request a quote, or book a call, we may collect:

  • Contact information: name, phone number, email address
  • Location information: ZIP code, and service address if you book an installation
  • Usage information: pages visited, time on site, and general interaction data, collected automatically via standard analytics and advertising tools

How we use your information

  • To provide your water hardness result and recommended package
  • To contact you about your quote, scheduled call, or booked installation
  • To send you information about our services, if you've opted in
  • To improve our website and advertising based on aggregate usage patterns
  • To measure the effectiveness of our advertising campaigns (see "Advertising and analytics" below)

Advertising and analytics

We use tools including Google Ads and Meta (Facebook/Instagram) advertising to reach potential customers and measure how well our ads perform. These tools may use cookies or similar technologies to:

  • Track whether someone who saw or clicked an ad later used our tool or requested a quote
  • Show you relevant ads on other websites and platforms based on your visit to our site

You can opt out of personalized advertising through Google's Ad Settings and Meta's Ad Preferences.

How we share your information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share it with:

  • Service providers who help us operate our business — our CRM/scheduling platform, and the installer who performs your service, when you book an appointment
  • Advertising platforms (Google, Meta) in aggregated or hashed form, for the ad measurement purposes described above
  • Legal authorities, if required by law

Your choices

  • You can ask us to delete your information by contacting us at the details below
  • You can opt out of marketing emails or texts at any time
  • You can decline to provide information, though this may limit your ability to use certain features (like getting a personalized quote)

Data security

We take reasonable measures to protect your information, but no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security.

Children's privacy

Our services are intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.

15gpg avgVery Hard
North Texas Municipal Water District

Plano, Frisco, Allen, McKinney and the rest of Collin County draw from the North Texas Municipal Water District. Lots of newer construction here, which surprises people — a five-year-old house scales up just as fast as an older one if the water coming in is hard.

Denton County

16gpg avgVery Hard
City of Denton + Upper Trinity Regional Water District

A mix of the City of Denton's own system and the Upper Trinity Regional Water District across the rest of the county. Denton is also where we see the most private wells in our service area alongside city water, so the right system depends genuinely on which one you're on.

15gpg avgVery Hard
North Texas Municipal Water District

The smallest county in Texas by area, and one of the fastest-growing. Almost everything here runs on North Texas Municipal Water District supply, and the housing stock is new enough that owners are often surprised how quickly scale shows up anyway.
